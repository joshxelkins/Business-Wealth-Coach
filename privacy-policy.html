Privacy Policy

How we handle your information.

Business Wealth Coach Ltd respects your privacy. This page explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we use it, and the choices you have.

Who we are

Business Wealth Coach Ltd is the controller for personal information collected through this website and through enquiries started from it.

What we collect

When you contact us or complete the onboarding form, we may receive your name, email address, WhatsApp number, business name, business type, package interest, turnover range and the message or details you choose to send. We also use privacy-friendly website analytics to understand page views and broad interaction events such as WhatsApp clicks, onboarding clicks and onboarding form progress. These analytics events do not include the personal answers you type into the onboarding form.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss whether we can work together.
  • To prepare for a discovery call or onboarding conversation.
  • To provide accounting, tax or business wealth coaching services if you become a client.
  • To keep the website secure, reliable and useful.
  • To understand which pages and calls to action are helping visitors, using aggregated website analytics.
  • To meet legal, regulatory and professional obligations.

Our lawful reasons

We usually use your information because you have asked us to take steps before entering into a contract, because we have a legitimate interest in replying to enquiries and running our business, because we need to meet legal obligations, or because you have given consent where that is required.

Who we share it with

We only share information where needed, such as with WhatsApp when you choose to message us, Calendly when you book a call, Resend when we send onboarding details by email from the website, Vercel as our website hosting, functions and analytics provider, professional advisers, accountancy systems, or authorities where required by law. We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information only for as long as needed to deal with your message and follow up appropriately. If you become a client, we keep records for the period required by law, tax rules and professional standards.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or restrict use of your personal information. You can also object to certain uses or ask for a copy of your information. You can raise a concern with the UK Information Commissioner's Office if you are unhappy with how your data is handled.

External links

This website links to WhatsApp, Calendly, Instagram and YouTube. Those services have their own privacy policies and may process your information when you use them.

Contact

To ask about this policy or your information, contact us using the WhatsApp links on this website.
