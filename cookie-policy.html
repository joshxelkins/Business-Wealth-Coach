Cookie Policy

Cookies, kept simple.

This website is intentionally light. Business Wealth Coach uses Vercel Web Analytics to understand visits and key actions without setting analytics, advertising or marketing cookies on your device.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that websites can place on your device. They can help a website work, remember choices, measure visits or support advertising.

Cookies we use

We do not currently use non-essential cookies on this website. The site is static, and the onboarding form prepares a WhatsApp message in your browser rather than storing your answers in a website database. Analytics events may record broad actions such as page views, WhatsApp clicks, onboarding clicks, form progress milestones and form submission intent, but not the personal details entered into the form.

Essential technology

Our website host, Vercel, may process basic technical information such as IP address, browser details, page URL, referrer, device type and broad location to deliver the site, protect it, keep it reliable and provide aggregated analytics. Vercel Web Analytics is designed to work without third-party cookies and is not used by Business Wealth Coach for advertising.

Third-party links

If you click through to WhatsApp, Instagram or YouTube, those services may use their own cookies or similar technologies. Their cookie and privacy settings are controlled by those platforms.

If this changes

If we add advertising pixels, marketing cookies or other non-essential cookies in future, we will update this policy and ask for consent where required.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Because this website does not currently set non-essential cookies, blocking cookies should not stop the main site from working.
