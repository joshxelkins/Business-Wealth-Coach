LEAVE THE HANDOVER TO US

Switching accountant?

Changing accountant is completely normal — and it does not need to be awkward or complicated.

Once you decide to move, we can handle the handover for you. We will liaise directly with your existing accountant, request the information we need, transfer the relevant records and systems, and make sure nothing gets lost in the process.

That can include things like:

  • Prior-year accounts and tax returns
  • Bookkeeping records
  • Payroll information
  • VAT records
  • Software access and accounting systems
  • HMRC and Companies House details
  • Ongoing deadlines and outstanding work

You should not have to spend your time coordinating the move between two firms. We will manage the transition, keep you updated, and make sure the switch is as smooth as possible.
