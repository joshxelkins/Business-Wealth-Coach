Cookie Policy

Cookies, kept simple.

This website is intentionally light. At the moment, Business Wealth Coach does not set analytics, advertising or marketing cookies on your device.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that websites can place on your device. They can help a website work, remember choices, measure visits or support advertising.

Cookies we use

We do not currently use non-essential cookies on this website. The site is static, and the onboarding form prepares a WhatsApp message in your browser rather than storing your answers in a website database.

Essential technology

Our website host may process basic technical information, such as IP address and browser details, to deliver the site, protect it and keep it reliable. This is not used by Business Wealth Coach for advertising.

Third-party links

If you click through to WhatsApp, Instagram or YouTube, those services may use their own cookies or similar technologies. Their cookie and privacy settings are controlled by those platforms.

If this changes

If we add analytics, advertising pixels or other non-essential cookies in future, we will update this policy and ask for consent where required.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Because this website does not currently set non-essential cookies, blocking cookies should not stop the main site from working.
